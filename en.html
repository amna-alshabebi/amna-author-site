About the Author

Amna Majid AlShabebi is an Iraqi writer and novelist based in the United Arab Emirates. Her first book, Hawamish Lam Tuthkar (Margins Left Unmentioned), was published in 2024, followed by the social novel Aabaa Yahsaboon Annahum Yuhsinuna Sun'a in 2026 by Ramsatna Publishing & Distribution. Her works have participated in book fairs and literary events in the UAE and abroad, and she has appeared in television interviews discussing her books and literary experience. She is a member of the Arabic Language Protection Association and the Fujairah Cultural Association.

2026 · Social novel · Ramsatna Publishing & Distribution

آباء يحسبون أنهم يحسنون صنعا

Aabaa Yahsaboon Annahum Yuhsinuna Sun'a is a social novel exploring upbringing and emotional listening within the family, asking what happens when material needs are met while reassurance and emotional communication between parents and children are absent.
